Centerpiece · the decision

"Should we build a custom RCA agent?"

For the IOH Catalyst, the original design proposed a five-agent architecture with a custom-built RCA agent at its core:

Proposed design

Take a general-purpose LLM → give it Datadog access via MCP tool calls → query metrics, logs, traces → reason to a root cause in 45 seconds.

The intent was right. The implementation had real gaps.

Why I challenged it

Five gaps a demo would hide and production would expose

Not trained for RCA

A general model can't tell a root cause from a correlated symptom, and has no knowledge of IOH topology. Every investigation starts from zero.

No way to know if it's right

No eval dataset, no accuracy target, no wrong-answer detection. A confident wrong diagnosis sends engineers down the wrong path.

RAG ≠ reasoning

Retrieving a similar past incident tells you what happened — not why this one is happening, or whether the pattern even applies.

The 45s claim was unbenchmarked

An assumption presented as a spec. No test behind it.

Build cost dwarfs the estimate

Datadog's RCA model Toto was trained on 1T+ observability datapoints at ~$750K compute, through thousands of structured evals. One enterprise on a programme timeline can't close that gap.

The call

Don't build the agent. Build the system around one.

Use a purpose-built investigation capability (Bits AI SRE) for diagnosis — and redirect the team's effort to what genuinely needed building for IOH: the pipeline · integration · governance · feedback loop.

Traded away
  • "We built our own agent"
  • Full control of the RCA component
Gained
  • Accuracy from day one — validated model
  • A 45s target that's testable, not assumed
  • A predictable live demo
  • A more credible TM Forum submission

Decisions & trade-offs

The honest calls under real constraints

Instrumentation

COTS you don't own

OTel where exposed; binaries you can't touch (Siebel); agents hidden in app UIs (Tibco). Triage by instrumentability up front; scope honestly.

Performance

Telco overhead

Agents/tracers add CPU + latency; perf testing is mandatory. Balance capacity vs depth; discipline on debug logs & profilers in prod PoVs.

Governance

Security-gated SLA

Sev1 = 15m response · 1h restore · 1d resolve. Critical vuln with no fix auto-blocks deploy. Slower delivery, enforced safety.

Outcome

Closed-Won — and positioned for the next play

Reflections

What I'd keep, change, and what it taught me

Went well

The RCA build-vs-buy reframe; reframing incident management for the CIO as reactive→proactive in business terms; keeping IOH's trusted partners inside the deal.

Do differently

Align with the customer's IS function earlier; triage COTS instrumentability before the PoV; get ahead of SAST accuracy concerns proactively.

Learned

In a managed-services-led enterprise, adoption is the product. And the senior move on AI was not building the model — it was owning the integration and governance around a validated one.
