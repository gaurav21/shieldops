Use Case

Autonomous Security Remediation

Scanner finds a CVE. Devin reads the advisory, locates every affected call site, applies the fix, runs the tests, and opens the PR. Hours instead of weeks.

The Problem

Your scanners are fast. Your fixes are slow.

SAST, SCA, Fortify, Veracode, SonarQube — they all find vulnerabilities in minutes. But fixing them takes weeks: triaging, assigning, context-switching, testing, reviewing. The backlog grows faster than your team can close it.

Dependabot and Renovate bump version numbers — but when the build breaks (and it will on major upgrades), they stop. The hard part — understanding breaking changes and fixing call sites — is left to your engineers.

Itaú benchmark: ~70% of SonarQube, Fortify, and Veracode security findings resolved automatically by Devin. 300,000+ repos documented with continuously updated documentation.

What Devin Does Differently

Reads CHANGELOGs. Fixes code. Ships green.

  • Reads the advisory — CVE description, CHANGELOG, migration guide, not just the version number
  • Finds every call site — across the entire codebase, not just the import statement
  • Applies the fix — refactors deprecated APIs, updates patterns, handles edge cases
  • Runs tests — full test suite, not a spot check. Iterates on failures until green
  • Opens PR — with full diff, rationale, test results, and confidence context
  • Handles breaking changes — the part Dependabot, Renovate, and Snyk can't do

500 findings × 4h avg = 2,000 engineer-hours
With Devin: 500 PRs × 10min review = 83 engineer-hours · 24× reduction

Devin understands what OCC examiners actually look for. The generated tests validate:
